# NNNN. Short decision title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](xxxx-....md) | Deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, project, constraints? What problem is being solved?
State the facts neutrally; the decision should feel inevitable given the context.

## Decision

The change being made, stated in full sentences with an active voice ("We will ...").

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include trade-offs accepted and follow-ups,
not just the upside.
