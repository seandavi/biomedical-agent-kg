Agent Laboratory is an autonomous research workflow designed to assist human researchers by automating repetitive tasks such as literature reviews, experiment execution, and report writing. Built by [[org:samuelschmidgall]], the system utilizes a multi_agent architecture to facilitate collaboration across the research lifecycle, aiming to accelerate [[domain:scientific_discovery]].

The platform is available as a cli and a library, allowing users to integrate specialized agents that leverage large language models to manage complex research objectives. Additionally, the system supports AgentRxiv, a framework that enables autonomous agents to share, retrieve, and build upon previous research to achieve cumulative progress.
