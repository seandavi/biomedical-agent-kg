SOP-Agent is a single_agent framework designed to enhance the practical application of large language models by integrating domain-specific knowledge through pseudocode-style Standard Operational Procedures. By representing these procedures as decision graphs, the system guides agents through complex, long-horizon tasks that require structured reasoning and human expertise.

The framework demonstrates versatility across diverse domains, including decision-making, code generation, and data cleaning. To evaluate these capabilities, the system introduces the Grounded Customer Service Benchmark, which assesses how effectively agents utilize SOPs to perform grounded tasks in customer service environments.
