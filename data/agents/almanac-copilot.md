Almanac Copilot is a single_agent system designed to assist clinicians with electronic medical record navigation, including information retrieval and order placement. By automating routine documentation tasks, the agent aims to reduce clinician burnout and improve the efficiency of clinical workflows.

The system is accessed via a web_ui and focuses on tasks related to [[domain:clinical_qa]]. In evaluations using the EHR-QA dataset, Almanac Copilot achieved a 74% task completion rate, demonstrating its potential to mitigate the cognitive load associated with current electronic medical record systems.
