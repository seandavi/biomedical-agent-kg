ChatNT is a single_agent multimodal conversational system built by [[org:ruisiang]] to bridge the gap between foundation models and biological sequence analysis. It provides an accessible web_ui for users to perform tasks involving DNA, RNA, and protein sequences without requiring coding expertise.

The system is designed to generalize across various biological processes and species, achieving state-of-the-art performance on the [[benchmark:nucleotide-transformer]] benchmark. It utilizes a genomics instruction-tuning framework and targets the [[domain:genomics_db]] to facilitate advanced biological comprehension.
