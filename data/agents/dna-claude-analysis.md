DNA Claude Analysis is a single_agent system built by [[org:shmlkv]] that enables users to interpret genetic data through natural language conversation. By processing raw data from services like 23andMe, AncestryDNA, and Nebula, the system allows users to perform interactive explorations of their genome, ranging from health and nutrition to ancestry and cognitive traits.

The tool exposes both a cli and a web_ui to facilitate analysis, which targets [[domain:genomics_db]] information. Users can execute specialized scripts to investigate specific markers, receive plain-English explanations of complex genetic results, and engage in deep-dive research regarding their specific genotype and associated scientific literature.
