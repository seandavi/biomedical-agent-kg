PRIME is a multi-agent system designed to streamline complex computational workflows within the field of [[domain:protein_structure]]. By utilizing a library of over 65 validated tools, the system interprets high-level engineering objectives to autonomously construct and execute custom pathways, reducing the need for specialized manual orchestration.

The architecture prioritizes reliability by grounding every step in verifiable tool execution to mitigate model hallucinations. Through this approach, PRIME automates end-to-end pipelines—from data acquisition to model training—to facilitate advancements in [[domain:scientific_discovery]].
