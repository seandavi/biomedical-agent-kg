MAGDA is a multi-agent system designed to provide diagnostic assistance through the application of clinical guidelines. By leveraging a multi-agent architecture, the system coordinates specialized processes to support complex medical reasoning tasks.

The platform exposes a web_ui to facilitate user interaction and workflow management. MAGDA specifically targets [[domain:clinical_qa]] to improve the accuracy and reliability of diagnostic support in healthcare environments.
