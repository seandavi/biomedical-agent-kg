ProtAgents is a multi_agent platform designed to facilitate de novo protein design through the collaborative efforts of specialized AI agents. By leveraging large language models, the system integrates diverse capabilities including knowledge retrieval, physics-based simulations, and comprehensive data analysis to address complex objectives in [[domain:scientific_discovery]].

The system operates within a dynamic environment where agents work synergistically to analyze [[domain:protein_structure]] and generate new first-principles data. This collaborative framework enables the automated design of proteins with targeted mechanical properties, providing a flexible approach to multi-objective materials problems.
