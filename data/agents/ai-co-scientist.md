The AI co-scientist is a multi-agent, self-evolving system built by [[org:the-swarm-corporation]] to augment the process of [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. By utilizing a generate, debate, and evolve methodology, the system assists researchers in formulating novel hypotheses and research proposals. It is available as a library and leverages an asynchronous task execution framework to scale compute for complex scientific inquiries.

The architecture employs a tournament-based evolution process to iteratively refine hypotheses through peer review, ranking, and meta-analysis. The system has been validated in biomedical domains, including drug repurposing, novel target discovery, and the study of bacterial evolution, demonstrating the ability to generate actionable insights and recapitulate experimental findings.
