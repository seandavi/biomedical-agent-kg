DORA AI Scientist is a multi-agent system designed to streamline the research process through automated and semi-automated workflows. By utilizing hierarchical teams of AI agents, the platform assists researchers with tasks ranging from [[domain:literature]] review and data processing to experimental design and the generation of academic paper drafts.

The system leverages generalist and domain-specific large language models to support [[domain:hypothesis_gen]] and facilitate [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. Accessible via a web_ui, DORA aims to reduce the manual effort required for manuscript preparation, allowing users to focus on high-value research activities.
