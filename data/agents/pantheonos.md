PantheonOS is an evolvable, distributed multi-agent framework developed at Stanford in 2025 and built by [[org:aristoteleo]]. The system is designed to automate and scale data science workflows, specifically targeting [[domain:single_cell]] biology and [[domain:spatial]] analysis. It utilizes agentic code evolution to optimize algorithms and supports various team orchestration patterns, including swarm and mixture-of-agents architectures.

The platform exposes its capabilities through an api, cli, library, and web_ui. Users can access a community marketplace containing over 1,000 curated agents and skills, facilitating specialized scientific tasks through a privacy-preserving, fault-tolerant infrastructure.
