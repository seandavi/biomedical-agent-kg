Biomni is a general-purpose biomedical AI agent built by [[org:stanford-university]] to autonomously execute complex research workflows. Utilizing a single_agent architecture powered by rag and code-based execution, the system dynamically composes tasks without relying on predefined templates. It is accessible via a library and a web_ui.

The agent targets a broad spectrum of research areas, including [[domain:hypothesis_gen]], [[domain:perturbation]], [[domain:single_cell]], [[domain:clinical_qa]], [[domain:drug_discovery]], and [[domain:literature]]. By integrating LLM reasoning with multi-modal data interpretation, Biomni aims to enhance scientific productivity and generate experimentally testable protocols.
