Biomni is a general-purpose biomedical AI agent developed by [[org:stanford-university]]. Utilizing a single_agent architecture powered by rag and code-based execution, the system is designed to assist scientists in automating research tasks and generating testable insights.

The platform targets research applications in [[domain:hypothesis_gen]], [[domain:perturbation]], and [[domain:single_cell]] analysis. Users can access the system via a provided library or through its web_ui.
