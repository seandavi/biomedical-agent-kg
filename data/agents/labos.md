LabOS is a multi-agent, self-evolving system designed to function as an AI co-scientist. By integrating multimodal perception with Extended-Reality-enabled collaboration, the platform connects AI agents, smart glasses, and robotics to bridge the gap between computational reasoning and physical experimentation.

The system exposes a web_ui to facilitate real-time human-AI interaction within the laboratory. By enabling AI to perceive experimental contexts, LabOS supports complex research tasks, including stem-cell engineering, material science, and [[domain:scientific_discovery]] for cancer immunotherapy targets.
