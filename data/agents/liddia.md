LIDDIA is a single_agent system designed to autonomously navigate the complex process of [[domain:drug_discovery]]. By leveraging the reasoning capabilities of large language models, the system functions as an adaptable tool for in silico research, aiming to expedite the identification of potential therapies.

The agent is implemented as a library and has been evaluated on its ability to generate molecules that meet pharmaceutical criteria across various clinically relevant targets. LIDDIA demonstrates an intelligent balance between exploration and exploitation within chemical space, successfully identifying novel candidates for critical targets such as AR/NR3C4.
