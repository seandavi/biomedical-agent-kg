BIA (BioInformatics Agent) is a system designed to leverage large language models to optimize and reshape bioinformatics workflows. It aims to streamline complex computational biology tasks by integrating advanced language processing capabilities into the research pipeline.

By focusing on the automation and enhancement of bioinformatics processes, BIA seeks to improve efficiency for researchers handling biological data. The system is engineered to address the specific challenges inherent in modern bioinformatics analysis.
