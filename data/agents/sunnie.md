Sunnie is a single_agent LLM-based conversational system designed to provide personalized mental well-being intervention activity recommendations. The system utilizes an anthropomorphic interaction design, incorporating a specific persona, appearance, and natural conversation style to engage users.

Accessible via a web_ui, Sunnie has been evaluated for its ability to meet and exceed user expectations regarding utility. Research indicates that the system’s anthropomorphic design is particularly effective at fostering relational warmth, outperforming non-anthropomorphic baselines in mental health support contexts.
