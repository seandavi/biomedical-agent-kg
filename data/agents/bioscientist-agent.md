BioScientist Agent is an end-to-end framework designed to address the inefficiencies of traditional [[domain:drug_discovery]]. By integrating a billion-fact biomedical knowledge graph with a multi-agent LLM architecture, the system facilitates systematic drug repurposing and the elucidation of mechanisms of action.

The agent utilizes a variational graph auto-encoder for link prediction and a reinforcement learning module to identify biologically plausible pathways. This modular design supports automated causal reporting and accelerates [[domain:hypothesis_gen]], providing researchers with evidence-based insights to reduce experimental overhead in early-stage development.
