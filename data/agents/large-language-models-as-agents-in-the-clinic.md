Large Language Models as Agents in the Clinic explores the transition of LLMs from passive information processors to intelligent agents capable of influencing clinical decision-making. Utilizing a multi_agent architecture, these systems are designed to engage in open-ended conversations and interact within complex healthcare workflows.

The framework emphasizes moving beyond standardized testing toward real-world performance assessments, specifically targeting [[domain:clinical_qa]]. By employing high-fidelity simulations and Artificial-intelligence Structured Clinical Examinations, the system aims to evaluate agent self-governance and dynamic interactions to ensure safe deployment in clinical environments.
