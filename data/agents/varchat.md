VarChat is a generative AI assistant designed to streamline the interpretation of human genomic variations. Built by [[org:varkentin]], the system utilizes a rag architecture to search and synthesize fragmented scientific [[domain:literature]] into concise, clinically relevant summaries.

Accessible via a web_ui, the tool provides detailed descriptions of genetic variants, their impact on protein function, and potential implications for human health. By connecting users directly to trusted scientific sources, VarChat facilitates efficient clinical assessment and reporting.
