SciMon (Scientific Inspiration Machines Optimized for Novelty) is a modeling framework designed to enhance the generation of novel scientific ideas grounded in existing [[domain:literature]]. Developed by researchers at the [[org:university-of-illinois-urbana-champaign]], the [[org:allen-institute-for-artificial-intelligence]], and the [[org:hebrew-university-of-jerusalem]], the system utilizes a rag architecture to retrieve past scientific papers as inspirations for new research directions.

By iteratively comparing generated ideas against prior work, the system explicitly optimizes for novelty to support [[domain:hypothesis_gen]] and broader [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. SciMon is available as a library, providing a structured approach to overcoming the limitations of traditional link prediction models by producing grounded, natural language suggestions.
