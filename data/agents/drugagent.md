DrugAgent is a multi-agent system designed to enhance the accuracy and interpretability of drug-target interaction prediction within the field of [[domain:drug_discovery]]. By utilizing a coordinator-based architecture, the system integrates specialized perspectives from machine learning predictions, knowledge graphs, and literature evidence to provide transparent, human-interpretable reasoning for its outputs.

The system incorporates Chain-of-Thought and ReAct frameworks to facilitate complex reasoning, ensuring that each prediction is supported by evidence from multiple sources. Available as a library, DrugAgent aims to support clinical decision-making and regulatory compliance by offering a reliable, multi-perspective approach to biomedical research.
