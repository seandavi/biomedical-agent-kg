ClinicalAgent is a multi-agent system designed to enhance the performance of large language models in clinical trial applications. By integrating GPT-4 with LEAST-TO-MOST and ReAct reasoning technologies, the system addresses limitations in external knowledge access to improve predictive accuracy and utility in medical contexts.

The framework is specifically engineered to support tasks within [[domain:clinical_qa]]. It demonstrates competitive predictive performance in clinical trial outcome prediction, achieving a 0.7908 PR-AUC and outperforming standard prompting methods.
