SciAgents is a multi-agent framework built by [[org:openags]] designed to automate complex research tasks. By integrating large language models with ontological knowledge graphs and data retrieval tools, the system facilitates autonomous exploration and the identification of patterns within vast scientific datasets.

The framework functions as a library that supports modular agent and tool configurations, including ready-to-use notebook examples for research workflows. It specifically targets [[domain:scientific_discovery]] and [[domain:hypothesis_gen]], enabling the autonomous generation and refinement of research hypotheses to uncover interdisciplinary relationships and accelerate material science development.
