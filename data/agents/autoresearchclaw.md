AutoResearchClaw is a multi-agent, self-evolving system built by [[org:aiming-lab]] to facilitate autonomous research. Designed to support both fully autonomous workflows and human-in-the-loop collaboration, the system enables users to generate research papers by providing a topic or idea. It is evaluated on [[benchmark:arc-bench]] and is engineered to target advancements in [[domain:scientific_discovery]].

The platform exposes both a cli and a web_ui to accommodate different user workflows. It features domain-specialist execution agents capable of handling research tasks across various fields, including biology, mathematics, and computing.
