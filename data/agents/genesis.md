Genesis is a multi-agent system designed to automate the closed-loop cycle of systems biology research. By integrating hardware such as computer-controlled $\mu$-bioreactors and the AutonoMS platform for high-throughput mass spectrometry, the system aims to execute one thousand hypothesis-led experiments per day to improve models with thousands of interacting causal components.

The infrastructure includes Genesis-DB for structured data access and RIMBO for managing model revisions. Through the application of the LGEM+ relational learning system, Genesis facilitates the automated abductive improvement of genome-scale metabolic models, advancing the broader goal of [[domain:scientific_discovery]].
