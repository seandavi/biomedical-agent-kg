BORA is a Language-Based Bayesian Optimization Research Assistant designed to address high-dimensional, non-convex optimization challenges. Built by [[org:weixiang-sun]], the system utilizes a single_agent architecture to integrate Large Language Models with Bayesian optimization, providing real-time commentary and reasoning to guide experimental searches.

The framework is accessible via a cli and specifically targets [[domain:medical_imaging]] and other experimental tasks. By blending stochastic inference with domain-specific insights, the agent aims to mitigate human confirmation bias and improve exploration efficiency in complex search spaces.
