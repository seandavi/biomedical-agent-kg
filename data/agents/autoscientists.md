AutoScientists is a multi-agent system built by [[org:harvard-medical-school]] designed for long-running computational scientific experimentation. The system utilizes a decentralized architecture where AI agents self-organize into teams to evaluate hypotheses, critique proposals, and share findings to optimize research trajectories. It is accessed via a cli and coordinates through a local server.

The system targets research across [[domain:drug_discovery]], [[domain:medical_imaging]], [[domain:protein_structure]], and [[domain:single_cell]]. Its performance has been evaluated on [[benchmark:bioml-bench]] and [[benchmark:proteingym]], demonstrating improvements in biomedical machine learning tasks and fitness prediction assays.
