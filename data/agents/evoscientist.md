EvoScientist, built by [[org:evoscientist]], is a multi_agent and self_evolving system designed to facilitate [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. It operates as a human-on-the-loop research partner, enabling AI to autonomously generate insights and iteratively improve its own scholarly judgment and research capabilities.

The system exposes a cli, mcp, skills, and web_ui to support its functionality. It has been evaluated on [[benchmark:deepresearch-bench-ii]] and [[benchmark:astabench]] to assess its performance in scientific research tasks.
