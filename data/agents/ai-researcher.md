AI-Researcher is an autonomous system built by [[org:hkuds]] designed to facilitate [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. Utilizing a multi_agent architecture, the platform provides end-to-end research automation, supporting workflows that range from detailed idea development to reference-based ideation. The system is evaluated on [[benchmark:scientist-bench]].

The platform exposes a web_ui to assist users with literature reviews, idea generation, and algorithm design and implementation. By streamlining the research process, AI-Researcher aims to accelerate scientific innovation through its integrated research ecosystem.
