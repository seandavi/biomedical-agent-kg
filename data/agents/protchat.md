ProtChat is a multi_agent system designed to automate complex protein analysis by integrating the inference capabilities of protein language models, such as ESM and MASSA, with the task-planning abilities of GPT-4. By streamlining workflows that previously required significant manual intervention, the system enables users to perform tasks like protein property prediction and protein-drug interactions through direct natural language instructions.

The platform exposes a web_ui to improve accessibility for researchers without a computational background. By automating these analytical processes, ProtChat facilitates advancements in computational biology and supports research initiatives within the field of [[domain:drug_discovery]].
