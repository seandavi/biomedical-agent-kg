Robin is a multi-agent system built by [[org:future-house]] designed to automate scientific discovery. It targets research in [[domain:drug_discovery]], [[domain:literature]], and broader [[domain:scientific_discovery]] workflows.

The system exposes a notebook interface to facilitate its operations. It supports hypothesis and experiment generation, with optional data analysis capabilities integrated through the Edison platform.
