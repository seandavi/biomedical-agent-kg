scAgent is a universal annotation framework designed to address the challenges of cellular heterogeneity in [[domain:single_cell]] RNA-seq data. Utilizing a single_agent architecture powered by Large Language Models, the system moves beyond fixed-type classification to enable generalization across diverse tissues.

The framework is capable of identifying known cell types and discovering novel ones with high data efficiency. Experimental results across 160 cell types and 35 tissues demonstrate its effectiveness in universal annotation and its extensibility to previously unseen cell types.
