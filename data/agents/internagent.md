InternAgent is a unified multi-agent framework built by [[org:alpha-innovator]] designed to conduct autonomous scientific research. By integrating human expert feedback and multi-agent interaction, the system facilitates end-to-end research processes that target [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. It is accessible through both a cli and a web_ui.

The system has demonstrated scalability and efficiency across 12 research tasks, achieving performance gains in fields such as reaction yield prediction and semantic segmentation. InternAgent has been evaluated on several benchmarks, including [[benchmark:mle-bench]], [[benchmark:gaia]], [[benchmark:hle]], [[benchmark:gpqa]], and [[benchmark:frontierscience]].
