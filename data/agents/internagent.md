InternAgent is a unified multi-agent framework built by [[org:alpha-innovator]] and [[org:internscience]] designed to facilitate autonomous [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. Utilizing a rag-enhanced architecture, the system supports end-to-end research workflows ranging from hypothesis generation to experimental execution across physical, biological, earth, and life science domains. It is accessible via api, cli, and web_ui interfaces.

The framework has been rigorously evaluated on benchmarks including [[benchmark:mle-bench]], [[benchmark:gaia]], [[benchmark:hle]], [[benchmark:gpqa]], [[benchmark:frontierscience]], and [[benchmark:mlebench]]. InternAgent emphasizes scalability, interactivity, and efficiency, enabling researchers to automate complex tasks such as algorithm discovery and empirical experimentation with significant time savings.
