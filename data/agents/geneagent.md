GeneAgent is a single_agent system designed to enhance gene set knowledge discovery in functional genomics. By integrating a self-verification module, the agent autonomously queries biological databases to validate information, effectively mitigating the hallucination risks typically associated with large language models.

The system demonstrates superior performance compared to standard models, providing reliable analytical narratives for complex genomic data. Through its application to novel gene sets, GeneAgent facilitates [[domain:scientific_discovery]] by offering expert-validated insights into gene functions and accelerating the research process.
