CRISPR-GPT is an agentic system designed to automate and enhance gene-editing workflows, including experiment planning, gRNA design, protocol drafting, and data analysis. By integrating domain-specific expertise with a specialized fine-tuned model, it facilitates complex task decomposition and decision-making to support [[domain:scientific_discovery]].

The system utilizes a multi_agent architecture combined with rag techniques to bridge the gap between general LLM reasoning and biological design requirements. Accessible via a web_ui, CRISPR-GPT functions as an AI co-pilot that enables interactive collaboration for researchers across various genome engineering modalities.
