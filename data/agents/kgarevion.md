KGARevion is a multi-agent, RAG-based system designed to address knowledge-intensive biomedical queries. By combining latent knowledge from large language models with grounded knowledge graphs, the agent generates and verifies triplets to ensure factual accuracy and contextually relevant reasoning.

The system is specifically engineered to support [[domain:clinical_qa]] tasks, demonstrating robust performance across diverse medical datasets. Its effectiveness has been validated through rigorous testing, including evaluations on [[benchmark:afrimed-qa]], where it exhibited strong zero-shot generalization capabilities in underrepresented healthcare contexts.
