MDAgents is a multi-agent framework designed to enhance medical decision-making by dynamically assigning collaboration structures to LLMs. By mimicking real-world clinical workflows, the system adapts to varying task complexities to synthesize multi-modal patient data and medical knowledge.

This approach improves diagnostic accuracy and supports adaptive responses in complex healthcare scenarios. By optimizing collaboration, MDAgents provides a more computationally efficient alternative to static multi-agent methods while serving as a tool for [[domain:clinical_qa]].
