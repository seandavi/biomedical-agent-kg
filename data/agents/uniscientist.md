UniScientist is a multi-agent, RAG-based system built by [[org:unipat-ai]] to advance [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. It utilizes an evolving polymathic synthesis paradigm to generate research-grade data across more than 50 scientific disciplines, employing an agentic research loop that integrates evidence acquisition, abductive inference, and report aggregation. The system is accessible via a cli and a library.

The model has been evaluated on [[benchmark:frontierscience-research]], [[benchmark:frontierscience-olympiad]], [[benchmark:deepresearch-bench]], [[benchmark:deepresearch-bench-ii]], and [[benchmark:researchrubrics]]. It demonstrates competitive performance in scientific problem-solving and research synthesis tasks.
