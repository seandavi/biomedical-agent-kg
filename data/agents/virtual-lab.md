Virtual Lab is a multi_agent system built by [[org:zou-group]] designed to facilitate AI-human collaboration in scientific research. The system enables human researchers to interact with teams of LLM agents through structured team and individual meetings to address complex research agendas.

The platform is available as a library and includes a notebook for implementing research workflows. It is specifically engineered to support [[domain:scientific_discovery]], as demonstrated by its application in designing and validating nanobodies for SARS-CoV-2.
