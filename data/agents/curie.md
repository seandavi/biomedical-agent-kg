Curie is a multi_agent framework built by [[org:just-curieous]] designed to automate the scientific research lifecycle. By providing an end-to-end system that handles hypothesis formulation, experiment implementation, execution, and result interpretation, it aims to enhance the rigor and reproducibility of scientific workflows.

The system is available as a library and targets [[domain:scientific_discovery]] and machine learning engineering tasks. Its performance and capabilities have been evaluated on [[benchmark:exp-bench]] and [[benchmark:mle-bench]].
