Medco is a multi-agent system designed to serve as a medical education copilot. By simulating a multi-disciplinary environment featuring an agentic patient, an expert doctor, and a radiologist, the framework facilitates interactive learning and collaborative training.

The system exposes a web_ui to support its educational objectives, which include improving question-asking skills and peer discussion. Medco specifically targets [[domain:clinical_qa]] to emulate real-world medical training and enhance the performance of virtual students.
