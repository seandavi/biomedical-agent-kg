Medco is a multi-agent system designed to serve as a medical education copilot. By simulating a collaborative environment featuring an agentic patient, an expert doctor, and a radiologist, the framework facilitates interactive learning and multi-disciplinary training.

The system focuses on developing proficient question-asking skills and peer discussion capabilities. It specifically targets [[domain:clinical_qa]] to emulate real-world medical training, demonstrating that simulated students can achieve performance enhancements and human-like learning behaviors through this collaborative approach.
