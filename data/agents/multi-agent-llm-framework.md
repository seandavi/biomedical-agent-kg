This multi-agent LLM framework is designed to support complex reasoning tasks within the biomedical field. By leveraging a multi-agent architecture, the system provides a scalable approach to assist researchers in [[domain:scientific_discovery]].

The framework is specifically engineered to facilitate [[domain:hypothesis_gen]], particularly in the context of drug combination discovery. It aims to augment human reasoning capabilities to streamline the exploration of biomedical research questions.
