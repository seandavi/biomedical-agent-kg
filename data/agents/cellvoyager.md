CellVoyager is a single_agent system designed to autonomously explore [[domain:single_cell]] datasets. By ingesting both raw data and records of prior research, the agent generates and tests new insights within a notebook environment, facilitating advanced [[domain:hypothesis_gen]].

The system has been evaluated on [[benchmark:cellbench]], where it demonstrated the ability to predict scientific analyses and generate novel, sound biological findings. By reanalyzing existing studies, CellVoyager identifies previously unexplored biological associations, acting as a collaborative tool to accelerate computational biology research.
