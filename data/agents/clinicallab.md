ClinicalLab is a single_agent system designed to align medical LLMs with real-world diagnostic practices. It addresses the limitations of existing evaluation methods by providing a comprehensive suite that focuses on multi-departmental clinical scenarios, specifically targeting [[domain:clinical_qa]].

The system utilizes [[benchmark:clinicalbench]], an end-to-end benchmark derived from real cases across 24 departments and 150 diseases. By incorporating ClinicalMetrics, ClinicalLab evaluates agent performance beyond simple multiple-choice questions, facilitating the development of ClinicalAgent to better meet the reliability requirements of modern medical practice.
