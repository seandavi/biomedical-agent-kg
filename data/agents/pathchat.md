PathChat is a single_agent biomedical LLM-based system designed to assist with [[domain:clinical_qa]]. Introduced in Nature Medicine 2024, the system provides specialized diagnostic support through a dedicated web_ui.

The platform was built_by [[org:mahmoodlab]] to facilitate expert-level interactions within the medical domain. It leverages its architecture to process complex clinical queries and provide evidence-based responses.
