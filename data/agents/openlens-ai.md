OpenLens AI is a fully autonomous multi_agent framework designed to streamline the health informatics research pipeline. By integrating specialized agents for tasks such as code generation, data analysis, and manuscript preparation, the system automates complex workflows while maintaining a focus on reproducibility and domain-specific quality requirements.

The platform features a web_ui and incorporates vision-language feedback to interpret medical visualizations. It is specifically engineered to synthesize [[domain:literature]] and generate publication-ready outputs, providing a traceable and transparent solution for end-to-end research execution in the biomedical sciences.
