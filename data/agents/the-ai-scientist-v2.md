The AI Scientist-v2 is a multi_agent system designed to automate the end-to-end process of [[domain:scientific_discovery]]. By utilizing a progressive agentic tree-search methodology and a dedicated experiment manager, the system autonomously formulates hypotheses, executes experiments, analyzes data, and authors manuscripts without the need for human-authored code templates.

Accessible via a cli, the system incorporates a Vision-Language Model feedback loop to refine the content and aesthetics of research figures. Its capabilities have been demonstrated through the successful submission of fully autonomous manuscripts to peer-reviewed workshops, marking a milestone in the application of AI to the research lifecycle.
