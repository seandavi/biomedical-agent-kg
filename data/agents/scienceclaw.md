ScienceClaw is a self-evolving AI research colleague built by [[org:beita6969]]. Designed specifically for academic researchers, the system targets [[domain:literature]] review and scientific analysis by maintaining persistent research memory across sessions. It utilizes a self-evolving architecture that allows the agent to adapt its capabilities to a user's specific field and research patterns over time.

The system exposes 285 initial skills, which expand at runtime as the agent learns from completed tasks. By integrating temporal decay weighting and vector storage, ScienceClaw supports long-duration research workflows, enabling the accumulation of domain-specific knowledge and the retrieval of past search strategies without the limitations of standard session-based AI assistants.
