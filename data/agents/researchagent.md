ResearchAgent is a multi_agent system designed to accelerate the pace of [[domain:scientific_discovery]] by automating the generation and refinement of research ideas. By leveraging the reasoning capabilities of large language models, the system identifies novel problems, proposes experimental designs, and iteratively improves these concepts through collaborative feedback.

The framework integrates information from academic graphs and knowledge stores to synthesize insights across diverse publications. It employs multiple reviewing agents, aligned with human preferences, to evaluate and refine proposed research, ensuring the generated ideas are both valid and impactful.
