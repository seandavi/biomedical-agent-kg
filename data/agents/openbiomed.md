OpenBioMed is an agent platform for biomedicine and life science built by [[org:pharmolix]]. Utilizing a multi_agent architecture, the system provides a comprehensive collection of skills designed to support complex research workflows. It exposes functionality through an api, notebook, skills, and a web_ui to facilitate user interaction and workflow customization.

The platform targets diverse research areas, including [[domain:clinical_qa]], [[domain:drug_discovery]], [[domain:protein_structure]], and [[domain:single_cell]] analysis. By integrating specialized toolkits and models, OpenBioMed offers end-to-end solutions for tasks ranging from molecular property prediction and protein design to omics data analysis.
